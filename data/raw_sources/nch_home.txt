Register your consumer grievance through the NCH Web Portal, Mobile App, UMANG, WhatsApp or Call Centre. NCH processes the grievance and forwards it to the concerned company, department or organization. The concerned entity reviews the grievance and provides a response or resolution update. Track your grievance status and view the latest response and resolution updates online. Need help with your grievance? Call National Consumer Helpline at 1915 or 1800114000
