# Deliberately excluded or unverified

- **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Amendment Rules, 2026**: the PDF is in `raw_sources/ecom2026_amend.pdf`.
  It changes rule 4(5) (adds a copy of the recorded complaint) and other rules. The extracted text lost the
  digits of the commencement date ("with effect from 1st January ..."), so I could not confirm when it applies.
  The 2020 text is used and the entries are flagged `volatile`.
- **e-Jagriti portal**: the site could not be fetched (scrape failed), so no portal guidance is included.
  Electronic filing is cited only via the proviso to Section 35(1) of the Act.
- **Filing fee slabs** (Consumer Protection (Consumer Disputes Redressal Commissions) Rules): not fetched, so no
  fee amounts are stated anywhere. The UI says fees are "as prescribed".
- **Case law**: none included by design. Any case citation in model output is removed by the verifier.
- **Section 34 base text says one crore**: superseded by the 2021 Rules (fifty lakh). Both are in the corpus; the
  Act entry carries a note pointing to the Rules entry.
